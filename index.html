HabiQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: HabiQ keeps your habits on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data. There is no account and no analytics.

What HabiQ stores

The habits you create, your daily check-ins, and your app settings (such as theme). All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. HabiQ has no user accounts, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, HabiQ asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

Backups and exports

When you choose Export backup or Export as spreadsheet, HabiQ creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where that file goes (for example Google Drive, your Files app, or email). Where you send it is governed by that service's own privacy policy. When you restore a backup, HabiQ only reads the file you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual habits in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling HabiQ or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

HabiQ does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If HabiQ adds features that handle data differently (for example optional paid features or cloud backup), we will update this page before those features are released and change the date above.
